Camp Flow
PHASE 1: Questionnaire
Objective:
  1. To gather essential health-related information from participants through a structured questionnaire.
  2. This phase aims to assess individual health risks, identify pre-existing conditions, and collect baseline data such as:
    • Age, gender, and lifestyle factors.
    • Current health concerns or symptoms.
PHASE 2: Testing and Lab Reports
Objective:
  • Conduct medical tests and gather lab reports to assess the physical health status of participants.
  • Provide accurate health assessments for each participant.
  • Identify any underlying health issues that may not have been detected during the questionnaire.
  • Generate lab reports that offer detailed insights into participants' health metrics.
